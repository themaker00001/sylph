What it is
A voice that disappears into your flow.

You already think faster than you type. Sylph closes the gap: one key, your voice, and clean text lands wherever your cursor is — an editor, a browser, a chat, a terminal. The whole engine lives on your machine, so it's instant and private by nature.

Fully on-device

Cactus Whistle runs locally. Your voice never leaves the machine — nothing is uploaded, ever.

~11 ms to first token

No network round-trip. The words are there before you lift your finger.

Private by design

Local history, zero telemetry, fully open source. Read every line that runs.

How it works
Four steps. No ritual.

Install, grant mic access, and talk. Under the hood, the webview captures audio and hands it to a resident Whistle model; the text is pasted back into whatever had focus.

01

Press the key

Double-tap your trigger key to go hands-free, or hold it to talk — your choice.

02

Just talk

A glyph-lit pill shows it's listening. Say what you mean, naturally.

03

On-device model

Whistle transcribes the audio locally in milliseconds — offline, every time.

04

It flows in

Text is pasted into the focused app — and kept in a scratchpad so it's never lost.

How we're building it
Small on purpose.

Most dictation apps ship a browser engine and phone your audio to a server. Sylph takes the opposite bet: a tiny native shell and a tiny on-device model. Here's the plan — lit nodes are shipped, hollow ones are next.

Shipped

Tauri shell, not Electron

The UI runs in the system webview via Tauri — a ~10 MB app instead of a bundled Chromium. Native Rust owns the hotkey, text insertion and the model bridge.

Shipped

Cactus Whistle, on-device

A 16.9 MB speech model that beats Whisper-base, resident in RAM via a Python sidecar. It downloads itself once on first launch, then never needs the network.

Shipped

A hotkey that feels right

Double-tap to toggle, hold to talk — on any key you choose, bound live with no restart. Built on global key-state polling so it's rock-solid and prompts for nothing.

Shipped

Scratchpad + invisible pill

Every transcript lands in a floating card you can copy from, and the pill can shrink to a single unobtrusive dot.

Next

One-click install

Freeze the Python sidecar with PyInstaller so the download runs with zero dependencies — no Python, no setup.

Next

Live streaming + Windows & Linux

Partial transcripts as you speak, and signed builds across all three desktops.
